I had a Reddit problem. Despite using it only in the browser and [redirecting it to old.reddit.com](https://addons.mozilla.org/en-US/firefox/addon/old-reddit-redirect/), I would find myself scrolling 20 minutes in for no apparent reason other than to see the same repost for the third time over. Greyscale, site time limits and even my [Doom Scroll user script](https://gitlab.com/sotilrac/doom-scroll) are all too easy to undo.

{% fig "/img/blog/buzzkill/modern-problems-chapelle.gif", "Modern problems require overkill solutions" %}

What I needed was real friction with real physical limitations. Turning off the phone requires a real amount of time to bring it back on, and I reckoned that maybe that would be enough to deter me. Of course, I could develop a habit of turning off the phone manually, but what I really needed was an excuse to make an Android app.

## Go for the Kill

{% fig "/img/blog/buzzkill/panel.png", "The panel. Corporations kill for your attention.", "18em" %}

BuzzKill powers the phone off. You set a kill zone, say 21:00 to 05:30, and inside it the app waits for the screen to stay off for a couple of minutes, then shuts the phone down. A sleeping phone becomes a dead one, and a dead one takes thirty seconds of boot animation to come back, which is long enough for the impulse to pass. Waking up at 2:00 to a buzz is impossible, because the phone is off. In the morning, the phone turns itself back on with Android's scheduled power-on functionality.

Android does not give apps permission to turn the phone off, so BuzzKill does it the way a person would: an accessibility service opens the system power dialog, finds the "power off" control by its text, and performs the slide gesture, with three retries if the slider does not engage. The strings it matches live in a per-vendor file, tuned for the OnePlus 15 I use (and the OnePlus 11 before it), so supporting another phone can be done without any code change. A test button runs the whole sequence in dry-run mode with a ten-second countdown, so you can check that the dialog still matches after a firmware update without losing whatever you had open.

The interface is one cyberpunk-inspired screen with retro notes à la Winamp: seven-segment displays for the kill zone, a toggle switch to arm it, a green LED when it is live.

A _hacker mode_ panel lets each day of the week have its own schedule. I ended up with the phone powering itself off after two idle minutes for most of my waking hours outside of work. It feels great!

## Killjoy

Reddit recently started requiring a login to use old.reddit.com. I do not log in, so the site simply stopped opening, and after a few months of BuzzKill the reflex to try had already weakened enough that this finished it. I guess I'm in the least valuable Reddit user tier.

BuzzKill is on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/ca.asmat.buzzkill/) and the [source is on GitLab](https://gitlab.com/sotilrac/buzz-kill). Cease the night!
